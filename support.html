What to include

  • Your Mikye Media order ID.
  • The username/handle you ordered.
  • The email used at checkout.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • Your payment reference if the issue is related to payment verification.

Order status

You can also use the Track order button on the Mikye Media homepage and enter your order ID to view the current order status.

Security

Never send Mikye Media your online-banking password, payment-provider password, full card credentials, or unrelated recovery codes. If you receive account-transfer information from Mikye Media, secure the delivered account immediately by replacing transferable credentials and recovery methods where available.

Before requesting a refund

If there is a problem with a paid order, contact support first so the payment, availability, and fulfillment history can be reviewed. Review the Refund & Replacement Policy for the situations in which a refund or replacement may be available.

Response time

Mikye Media aims to respond as promptly as reasonably possible. Response time can vary based on order volume, payment review, supplier availability, and the complexity of the issue.
