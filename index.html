FAQ

Before you buy

Is submitting the payment form proof that I paid?

No. Every payment is manually verified. A transaction reference helps locate the payment but does not guarantee approval.

How fast is delivery?

Delivery is manual and depends on payment verification and the underlying handle transfer. No instant-delivery promise is made.

What email should I use?

Use an email you can access. Approved transfer details and order updates are sent there.

Can availability change?

Yes. Short handles are limited inventory. If a handle becomes unavailable before verified fulfillment, the order must be resolved manually.
