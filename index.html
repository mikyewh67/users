FAQ

Before you buy.

Does submitting an order mean my payment is approved?+

No. Your payment reference helps locate the transaction, but payment is manually verified before fulfillment.

How fast is delivery?+

Delivery is manual and depends on payment verification and securing the selected handle. Transfer details are emailed when ready.

Why did a handle disappear?+

It may be reserved by another checkout, sold, or removed because availability changed.

Which email should I use?+

Use an email you actively control. Fulfillment details and important order updates are sent there.
