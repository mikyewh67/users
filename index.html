FAQ

Before you buy.

Quick answers about checkout, payment verification, and delivery.

Does submitting checkout mean my payment is approved?+

No. Every payment is manually verified. The transaction reference helps locate your payment but does not guarantee approval.

How quickly will I receive the handle?+

Fulfillment is manual and depends on payment verification and the underlying transfer. You will receive the transfer information by email once the order is ready.

Which email should I use?+

Use an email you actively have access to. Approved transfer information and order updates are sent there.

Can a handle become unavailable?+

Yes. Short usernames are limited inventory. If availability changes before verified fulfillment, the order is handled manually.
